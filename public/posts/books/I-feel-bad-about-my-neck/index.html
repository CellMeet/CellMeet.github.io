Chapter 2 | I Hate My Purse#

​ 今天读了Nora Ephron的I feel bad about my neck 的 第二篇， 读这篇文章（可以称为老掉牙）的时候， 今天也是Anthropic 上市的一天，她的朋友花了 2700 美元在 flea market 购买了一个“过时的”包。我只能苦笑，原来我如此贫穷😀, 同样地，作为一个AI 时代中的读者，读这样一篇文章，其实感觉是AI 也只是这个庞大的世界的一个小的“corner”。AI 应该不可能写出“I hate my purse”，算了，它实在太聪明了，是不会让自己有一个purse的！😂

​ 文章很简单，交代了自己为什么不喜欢purse, 里面东西太过杂乱，你可以用purse里面的东西逃过Cossacks 人，但是你却在里面什么都找不到！ 里面有各种各样的幽默，读的很开心。

​ I solved this problem by purchasing an overcoat with large pockets. This, I realize, turned my coat into a purse, but it was still better than carrying a purse. Anything is better than carry a purse.

​ “Perhaps the solution is not one purse but two. So I tried having tow purses, one for personal things and one for work things. (Yes, I know: The second purse is usually called a briefcase.)

​ you could flee the Cossacks with your purse. But when you open it up, you can’t find a thing in it , your purse is just like a big dark hole full of stuff that you spend hours fishing around for. A flashlight would help, but if you were to put one into your purse, you’d never find it.

​ “metamorphose into a monument to what happens to people who care too much about purses."

​ “What’s more , never having been in style, it can never go out of style."

​ “It doesn’t work for everything, I admit; on rare occasions”
